### What's Changed in v0.4.3

#### 🎨 Enterprise Sidebar & Navigation Ergonomics
- **Expanded Sidebar Layout**: Increased sidebar width to 288px (`w-72`) for enhanced legibility, natural line breaks, and comfortable viewing across desktop POS displays.
- **Enhanced Typography & Iconography**: Upgraded module menu buttons to 18px balanced icons and 13px typography with crisp active emerald indicators.
- **Brand Squircle Header**: Scaled the enterprise logo mark container with subtle ambient emerald illumination and live workstation status.
- **Solid Contrast Preservation**: Removed backdrop bleed to maintain 100% opaque navigation panels and contrast compliance.

#### 🏪 POS Terminal Status & Top Bar Refinements
- **Workstation Badging**: Updated the top status indicator to `Main Store · POS Terminal` for explicit cashier and store context.
- **Expanded Action Targets**: Refined collapse/expand toggles and profile actions with generous tactile touch/click bounds.
