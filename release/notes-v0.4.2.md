### What's Changed in v0.4.2

#### 💎 Enterprise Brand Assets & Desktop Icon Suite
- **Abstract Geometric Monogram**: Created a high-end corporate enterprise application icon featuring an interlocking 3D crystal prism forming the letter "M" and modular ERP hexagon, rendered on a dark titanium squircle tile with luminous emerald green and sapphire refractions. Replaces all placeholder and generic mobile phone clip-art.
- **Multi-Platform Icon Suites**: Auto-generated complete platform suites:
  - macOS: `icon.icns` high-DPI bundle
  - Windows: `icon.ico` and Store app tiles
  - Linux: 32x32, 64x64, 128x128, 256x256, and 512x512 PNGs
- **Web & Desktop Favicons**: Integrated `favicon.ico`, `favicon.png`, and `apple-touch-icon.png` in `index.html`.

#### 🎨 Executive Sidebar Redesign
- **Refined Navigation Styling**: Upgraded menu groups with sleek uppercase headers, subtle chevron animations, and modern pill navigation.
- **Active State Highlights**: Replaced the harsh vertical green border with an emerald pill highlight (`bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-700 ring-1 ring-emerald-500/25`) with active status dot.
- **Slim Custom Scrollbar**: Added thin, unobtrusive custom scrollbars (`scrollbar-width: thin`) eliminating the clunky raw browser scrollbars in the sidebar navigation.
- **Branded Header**: Embedded the master brand logo with ambient glow and enterprise tagline.

#### 🛡️ Sign-Out Safety Confirmation Modal
- **Accidental Logout Prevention**: Clicking "Sign out" now presents a confirmation dialog ("Confirm Sign Out", "Are you sure you want to sign out? Any unsaved edits will be discarded") with Cancel and Confirm actions.

#### 🏢 Executive Header & Terminal Status
- **Clean Top Bar**: Removed the "Offline ready" pill and replaced it with a store status indicator (`Main Store | Live Terminal`).
