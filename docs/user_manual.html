1. System Overview & Architecture

Mobile Shop Desktop ERP is a purpose-built desktop business management platform engineered specifically for retail mobile phone outlets, wholesale dealers, and repair service centers.

💡
Offline-First Architecture: The application does NOT depend on cloud connectivity. All transactions, IMEI searches, inventory updates, and repair tickets are processed instantaneously on your local computer via a lightning-fast SQLite database.

Key Highlights:

  • Serialized Inventory: Strict IMEI intake with duplicate detection for box-packed new phones and pre-owned devices.
  • Used Phone Compliance: Captures seller CNIC, phone number, and physical condition checks to protect your business legally.
  • Integrated Service Center: Job cards with advance deposits, physical fault checklists, and automatic spare-part inventory deductions.
  • Complete Double-Entry Ledgers (Khata): Tracks customer credit (udhaar), supplier balances, and running payment receipts.
  • Cash Shift Discipline: Morning drawer float entry, mid-day cash in/out, and end-of-day discrepancy reporting.

3. First-Time Setup & Store Configuration

  1. Log in as Administrator: Enter the initial admin PIN / password provided at installation.
  2. Configure Store Details: Navigate to Settings. Enter your Store Name, Contact Phone Numbers, Shop Address / City, Currency Symbol (e.g., Rs. or PKR), and Receipt Footer Notes.
  3. Add Branches (Optional): If operating across multiple outlets or warehouses, register each location under Branches.
  4. Create Staff Logins: Go to Staff & Roles, create individual logins for your cashiers and technicians, and assign their permissions.

4. Daily Cash Register & Shift Operations

The Cash Register module ensures strict cash accountability at the counter.

4.1 Opening the Register

  1. Open Cash Register at the start of your shift.
  2. Click Open Cash Session.
  3. Count and enter the physical Opening Float (cash left in the drawer for change, e.g., 10,000).
  4. Click Start Session. The POS is now enabled for sales.

4.2 Recording Mid-Day Cash Movements

  • Cash Out: Used when cash leaves the drawer for shop expenses (lunch, generator fuel, tea, couriers) or owner cash drawings.
  • Cash In: Used when extra cash float is deposited into the cash drawer.

4.3 Closing the Register & Reconciliation

Expected Cash = Opening Float + Cash Sales + Cash In - Cash Out - Cash Refunds
  1. At shift end, click Close Session.
  2. Count physical cash in the drawer and type into Physical Cash Counted.
  3. The system automatically computes whether the register is Balanced, Over, or Short.
  4. Confirm and print the shift summary.

5. Inventory & Stock Management

5.1 Box-Packed (New) Mobile Phones

Each new device requires scanning its 15-digit IMEI number. The system enforces unique IMEI protection so you can never accidentally enter the same phone twice.

  • Required fields: Brand & Model, Color/Variant, IMEI 1 & 2, Cost Price, Retail Price, Warranty Period.

5.2 Used / Second-Hand Phone Purchases

⚠️
Legal Compliance Rule: Always verify and enter the seller's complete National ID (CNIC) and active phone number when buying used devices.
  • Record battery health %, physical grade (A/B/C), and included original accessories (box, charger, receipt).
  • If full payment is not made upfront, the unpaid balance is automatically posted to the Seller Khata Ledger.

5.3 Spare Parts & Accessories

  • Spare Parts: Screens, batteries, charging ports, cameras. Set Minimum Reorder Level to receive low-stock alerts.
  • General Accessories: Scan manufacturer barcodes on packaging. Rapid checkout via USB barcode scanner.

6. Point of Sale (POS) & Billing

  1. Scan or Search: Aim your barcode scanner at the product barcode or type the IMEI into the POS search bar.
  2. Customer: Leave as Walk-in Customer or select an existing customer from your directory.
  3. Discounts: Enter item-level discounts or invoice-level discounts. The screen clearly highlights the subtotal, discount deducted, and net payable.
  4. Payment Method: Select Cash, Card, Bank Transfer, or Customer Credit (Khata).
  5. Print Invoice: Instant 80mm thermal receipt or full-page A4 invoice.

7. Customer & Supplier Ledgers (Khata Accounts)

Manage credit sales and supplier payouts without separate ledger notebooks:

  • Giving on Credit (Udhaar): Select customer at POS and choose Customer Credit. Invoice total is automatically debited to their account.
  • Receiving Balance Payments: Go to Khata / Customers, select the client, click Receive Payment, input amount, and print a payment receipt. Physical cash is instantly added to your active cash register.
  • Supplier Balances: View unpaid balances for used phone sellers or wholesale part distributors. Record payouts with one click.

8. Mobile Repair & Service Center Workflow

A complete 5-stage workshop lifecycle:

  1. Job Card Intake: Record customer contact, device model, passcode, problem description, physical condition checklist, estimated cost, and collect an Advance Deposit.
  2. Print Job Card Receipt: Print a receipt with ticket barcode and terms. The client gets one copy, and the workshop copy is attached to the phone.
  3. Technician Assignment: Move status from Pending to In Progress or Waiting for Parts.
  4. Consume Spare Parts: Select the part used (e.g., iPhone 13 OLED Screen). The part is automatically deducted from stock and linked to the repair cost.
  5. Final Settlement & Delivery: The system automatically deducts the advance deposit:
    Final Balance Due = Total Repair Cost - Advance Deposit Paid
    Collect final payment, close ticket, and print the warranty repair receipt.

9. Returns & Full IMEI Lifecycle Search

  • Customer Sales Returns: Scan receipt barcode, select return item, and choose refund mode (Cash, Exchange, or Store Credit).
  • IMEI Lifecycle Tracker: Enter any 15-digit IMEI to inspect its complete timeline: when purchased, from which seller, cost price, which customer purchased it, warranty status, and all repair history.

10. Expenses & Owner Drawings

  • Shop Expenses: Categorized logs for rent, electricity, tea/food, staff salaries, and stationery. Deducted from net operational profit.
  • Owner Drawings: Personal withdrawals by the store owner. Tracked cleanly in equity without distorting operating profit.

11. Business Reports & Analytics

  • Daily Sales & Profit: Real-time gross margin, itemized net profit, and revenue trends.
  • Inventory Valuation: Total capital invested across phones, spare parts, and accessories at current cost.
  • Technician Performance: Number of jobs finished and revenue generated per technician.
  • Low Stock Alerts: Fast-moving items requiring urgent reorder.

12. Database Backups, Restore & Maintenance

💾
Crucial Daily Rule: Create a backup to an external USB flash drive or cloud folder (Google Drive/Dropbox) every evening before shutting down your computer!

12.1 Creating a Backup

  1. Insert your USB drive.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Database Backup & Restore.
  3. Click Create Backup Now and select your USB drive folder.
  4. A verified, timestamped backup file (.db) is saved in seconds.

12.2 Restoring Database

To restore your data onto a new or formatted computer, click Restore Database, select your .db file, and confirm. All records are restored seamlessly.
